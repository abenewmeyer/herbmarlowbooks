How to use the Cover & Blurb Studio

Eight easy steps, from uploading your book to downloading your new cover. Take your time - nothing here can break your book, and everything you do is saved.

  1. Sign in
  2. Add books
  3. Pick one
  4. Analyze
  5. Your vision
  6. Cover & blurb
  7. Download
  8. Come back
Good to know: the computer drafts; you decide. Always read the summary, blurb and cover and change anything you don't like. Your manuscript is stored privately and only your account can see it.
1

Sign in and change your password

Open the website address Darren sent you. Type your email and the temporary password, then press Sign in.

  • The first time, a yellow bar asks you to change your temporary password. Press change it now and choose a new one with at least 10 characters.
  • Write the new password down somewhere safe.

If something looks wrong: Forgot your password? Ask Darren - he can reset it for you.

2

Drop in your books

At the top of the page, tick the box to confirm the books are yours, check your name in Author name, then drag your book files onto the dashed box. You can drop one book or many at once - Word (.docx), PDF or EPUB.

  • Each file becomes a book on its own. The title comes from the file name, and you can fix it later.
  • Wait until each line says "ready to analyze".
  • The PDF must have real, selectable text - not a scan or photo of pages.

If something looks wrong: A line says FAILED? Read the reason beside it - most often it is a scanned PDF. Upload the Word or EPUB version of that book instead.

3

Pick the book to work on

Under Your books you will see everything you have added. Type in the search box to find one, then click it. The book opens just below, ready for the next step.

  • The tag on the right shows where each book is: Ready to analyze, Summary to review, and so on.
  • Fix the title or author spelling in the boxes under the list - it is what prints on the cover.
  • Work on one book at a time. The free computer reading is limited, so books are analyzed one after another, not all at once.

If something looks wrong: Can't find a book? Clear the search box. To remove a book, press Delete this book (this cannot be undone).

4

Let the computer read the book

Press Analyze manuscript. It reads the book in small pieces and then writes a summary called the brief.

  • This takes 10-15 minutes. Leave the page open. If it says it is pausing, that is normal - it continues by itself.
  • When it finishes, read the summary. Fix anything that is wrong, press Save corrections, then Approve brief.

If something looks wrong: Closed the page by accident? Open it again and press Analyze - it picks up where it stopped.

5

Tell us what you picture

In the box, describe the cover you imagine: mood, colors, objects, setting. Then press Create cover concepts….

  • Example: A lone lighthouse at dusk, deep teal and amber, calm but a little haunting. No people.
  • You can change this later and try again.

If something looks wrong: Not sure what to write? Leave it short - you can refine it after you see the first covers.

6

Choose your cover and your back-cover blurb

On the left, read the three blurb versions (A, B, C). Click one, edit the words if you like, then press Approve this blurb. On the right, press Generate the 3 covers and click Use this cover under your favorite.

  • None close to your idea? Type what to change under a cover (for example "more fog, less orange") and press Regenerate closer to my idea.
  • Watch the counter at the top: it shows how many tries are left today. When it reaches 0 it resets the next day - or upload your own artwork instead.
  • Use older / newer under a cover to go back to an earlier try.

If something looks wrong: An image didn't come out? Your try is given back, so the counter doesn't go down.

7

Set the title and download your files

Under Design the type & export, check the title, author and colors on the preview. For a printed book, enter the final page count of your published paperback and pick the trim size. Tick the places you will publish, then press Validate & download ZIP.

  • The page count is the number of pages in the finished, formatted book - usually shown on the book's details page at your publisher.
  • Green/yellow notes are advice. A red BLOCKER tells you exactly what to fix before that file is produced.
  • The ZIP contains your cover file(s), the blurb as plain text, and marketing notes (tags, audiences) for your team.

If something looks wrong: Not sure which boxes to tick? Ask Darren - the ebook cover is the simplest one to start with.

8

Come back and change it any time

Everything is saved to your account automatically. Next time, sign in and choose your book under Open a book. In the design section, use Saved designs to keep named versions - for example "Blue lighthouse - serif" - and press Open to bring one back.

  • Change the title style, colors, blurb or page count, then download a fresh ZIP whenever you like.
  • Your old covers stay saved; use older / newer to browse them.
  • To remove a whole book, press Delete this project (this cannot be undone).

If something looks wrong: Nothing is published for you. You upload the downloaded files to the publisher's website yourself - Darren can help with that step.
